Before you write

Common questions.

Do I need a finished brief?

No. Many projects start as a rough problem statement. We'll help you shape scope, priorities, and a realistic first release.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. If your idea is sensitive, mention it in your message and we'll send a mutual NDA before you share details.

What size of project do you take on?

From focused engagements, such as an architecture review or an integration, to long-running product builds. If we're not the right fit, we'll say so and point you elsewhere.

Can you work with our existing team and systems?

Yes. We regularly join existing codebases and teams, and we document as we go so knowledge stays with you.

How soon can work start?

That depends on scope and current capacity. We'll give you a realistic start date in our first reply.
